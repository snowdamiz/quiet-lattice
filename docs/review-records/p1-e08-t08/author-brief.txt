## Readable sources

`assets/blend/creatures/<stem>.blend`

`assets/blend/constructs/outpost_drone.blend`, `outpost_overseer.blend`

Layout (`artlib/scene.py`, art bible 8):

- Metric, scale 1.0. One Blender unit is one metre. +Z up. Apply transforms.
- Walkers: origin between the feet, every sole on z = 0, facing −Y. Flyers and the two constructs: origin at the centre of mass. The drone hovers; its origin is the hover centre, not a buried foot.
- Top-level `REF`, hidden from export: `REF_Grid` (1 m, 20×20 m) and `REF_Biped` (1.85 m, feet at the origin).
- One top-level collection named the asset. Inside it, `<variant>_LOD0` through `<variant>_LOD3`.
- `COL_*` for one variant lives in that variant's collection, beside the LODs, not inside LOD0. A collider inside LOD0 is copied onto the generated LODs.
- Object names must not be the asset name, the variant id, or `<variant>_LOD<n>`.
- Collections starting with `_` are ignored. Link `assets/blend/_library.blend`. Do not edit it.

LOD0 is hand-authored. LOD3 is under 5% of that variant's LOD0. If a lower LOD collection is absent, stock export decimates at 1.0, 0.3, 0.1, 0.035. Decimation clears shape keys on the copies (`artlib/lods.py`). Do not use a shape key as the only copy of an attack pose: `export_morph=True` writes morphs, and the review scene never sets `morphTargetInfluences`, so a pose that exists only as a shape key is not a capture.

## Creature rig contract (do not change it)

The creatures family requires these point attributes on every render mesh (`families.json`): `_limb` FLOAT, `_pivot` FLOAT_VECTOR, `_glow` FLOAT. It also requires a colour-region mask. `geometry.quantize` is false for this family only: `_pivot` and the mask stay exact floats. Constructs do not carry these attributes, and their geometry is quantized. Do not put `_pivot` on a construct.

Limb ids, the same list in `Fauna.js` and `build_creatures.py`:

| Id | Part |
|---|---|
| 0 | body |
| 1 | front-left leg, or the biped's −X leg |
| 2 | front-right leg, or the biped's +X leg |
| 3 | hind-left leg |
| 4 | hind-right leg |
| 5 | tail |
| 6 | head and neck |
| 7 | −X wing, or a biped arm (DESIGN: crowd arms use the unused wing channels) |
| 8 | +X wing, or the other biped arm |

`_limb` is an integer stored as a float. `Fauna.js` does `Math.round` and the shader does `int(aLimb + 0.5)`. A fractional weight in `_limb` is destroyed by that consumer. Do not add a weight attribute, a skin modifier, or an armature. The creatures family does not ask for an armature; the bosses family does, and this task does not use it.

Hand-weighting is loop placement inside those integer ids. The walker shader bends a leg at half the hip height and blends that bend across vertices between 0.38 and 0.62 of the hip height, then levels the foot between 0.10 and 0.24 (`Fauna.js` `creatureMaterial`; the generator's `KNEE` is 0.5 and its ankle fraction is 0.13). Put the knee loop on that half-height band and the ankle loop in the foot band, with the leg a near-vertical column under its hip pivot, so the existing bend does not shear a rigid capsule. `_pivot` is the joint in Blender object space, Z-up, not converted to Y-up. On a posed variant, `_pivot` is that pose's joint, and the vertex ids stay the same ids as the rest variant.

`_glow` is 0 or 1 on small tips (eye catch, lure, horn tip). It is not a stone-touched hide. Glyph light `#D8F6FF` / `#7FD8FF` is the monolith network. These meshes do not use it, and they do not grow the dark crystal clusters the calibration called out on `quad_heavy_0`.

Colour mask, POINT domain, the active colour attribute, and the first colour attribute, render index 0: R primary, G secondary, B accent, weights summing to 1 (the check fails under 95%), A occlusion. Eyes use a dark pupil in A. The review and the turntable replace a creature's material with the dun game look (`packages/shared/src/art/looks.ts`: `#8A6B4F`, `#D8C6A0`, `#2F2622`, roughness 0.65, metalness 0.05, albedo cap 0.75, ao floor 0.05) wherever a colour attribute exists. Painted maps still have to pass the PBR profile, and the grader's stock images show the mask fill. Put the readable regions in the mesh and the mask. Do not delete the mask to force the baked maps on screen.

## Scale, class, materials

Creature class (`families.json`): LOD0 15,000–40,000 triangles, longest side 0.2–80 m inclusive, 4 LODs, 2048 maps, 128 MB per variant. The art-bible bands are not what the size check measures. The check is the longest side of LOD0 render meshes. Author to the band and stay inside 0.2–80:

| Plan | Band (art bible 1) | Rest pose |
|---|---|---|
| `quad_small` | shoulder 0.4–1.2 m | herd pair plus the pack predator |
| `quad_grazer` | shoulder 1.2–2.4 m | grinding jaw, wide-set prey eyes; no ball-tipped antennae |
| `quad_heavy` | shoulder 2.5–5 m | armoured brow and horn bosses; two herd silhouettes, no predator mesh |
| `biped` | 1.6–2.6 m tall | two herd silhouettes in that band; `biped_predator` pitched forward, counterweight tail, long clawed forelimbs, a jaw that opens |
| `flyer` | span 1–6 m, wings along ±X | two herd silhouettes; `flyer_predator` swept raptor wings and talons, not a flat moth |

The demo's uniform 1.40 m quads are the calibration failure. Do not match them.

PBR maps for the class: base colour, roughness, metallic, normal, occlusion, and an emissive map when the material emits. Share one 2k set across the variants of a file. A uniform map fails. Shipped export is KTX2 plus meshopt. Do not pass `--no-compress` into `packages/client/public/models`.

Attack poses, each a full variant at the same body size as its rest sibling:

- charge: forequarters extended, hind legs driving
- stomp: a forefoot raised or driven down
- kick: one hind leg extended
- bite_swarm: head forward, jaws open, one animal
- leap: forelimbs reaching, hind legs extended, body not translated by the 12 m gameplay close
- claw_combo: one forelimb extended
- dive: wings swept, talons forward
- screech: head raised, mouth open

`COL_*` one box per variant, under 512 triangles (over 512 warns). The creatures family does not require it. The review overlay draws it. The current fauna pick is a sphere and does not read `COL_*`; T07 is the adoption. No `Interact`. No `Door*` (any `Door*` in the file must be at least 2.4×1.4 m). No `Socket_*`, `HP_*`, `Mount`, or `Spin`.

## Schema facts the author has to satisfy

These do not need a `families.json` edit.

1. Body plans and the two machines are different families. Creature attributes and the colour mask are required on creatures and forbidden in spirit on constructs: a construct is quantized, so a `_pivot` there would not stay exact. The Overseer at 24 m and the drone at about 1.5–3 m both fit 0.2–80 m. Naming the Overseer `*warden*` would move it to hero (60,000–150,000 triangles, 4k maps). Do not.
2. The creature triangle floor is 15,000 per variant, including every attack pose and the drone. A smooth body or a subdivided box fails the floor or the placeholder rubric. The Overseer ceiling is 40,000 on a 24 m machine.
3. The validator's size test will not catch a 1.4 m heavy quadruped. The art-bible shoulder, height, and span bands will, at grade time. Hit the band.
4. Fractional `_limb` values are not weights. The consumer rounds them. Hand-weighting is the knee and ankle loops described above. No armature, no extra attribute.
5. Attack poses are variant meshes. A shape key is exported and then dropped from generated LODs, and the review scene shows the rest pose only. One posed variant per attack id is the copy the capture actually opens.
6. The creatures review image is the dun mask fill, not the baked PBR. Maps are still required, non-uniform, and at most 2048. The mask stays.
7. `COL_*` over 512 triangles warns. Keep colliders as boxes, one per variant, outside LOD0.
8. `Light_*` without `color`, `intensity`, and `distance` fails when the empty exists. `Blink` without a child mesh fails. Any `Door*` must be at least 2.4×1.4 m; these files have none.
9. Size is LOD0 render meshes only, inclusive (`lo <= val <= hi`). A wingtip or a laser past the band fails the grade even when it is inside 80 m.
