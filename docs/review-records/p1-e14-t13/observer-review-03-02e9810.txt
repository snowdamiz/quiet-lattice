Refusal therefore cannot depend on the clone filling holes.

Direct `validateObserverPreparation` / `validateObserverControl`, with no clone in front:

- Inherited pass index is readable and still throws `Missing preflight passes`.
- All-holes, middle hole, explicit `undefined`, and explicit `null` passes throw `Missing preflight passes`.
- Sparse jitter, sparse history, a hole in a history dimension, and an inherited jitter index throw `Malformed temporal observations`.
- Poll, proof-point, actual-byte, viewport, and sample holes throw.
