The only newer site consumer is unmerged and still owned as T10 source-complete. On `ea58f401`, `DEN_MODELS` is burrow, nest, roost, and apex only. `loadLibrary('structures')` fetches those seven `/models/sites/${id}.glb` files into a `dens` library.
