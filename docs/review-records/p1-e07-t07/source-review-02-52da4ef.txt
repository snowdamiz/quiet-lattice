R2-2 — P2: overdue scheduled ticks undo the wake clock and backdate fresh shield damage.

Primary location: `packages/server/src/zones/manager.ts:229`. Supporting locations: `manager.ts:391` / `:432`; `packages/server/src/zones/zone.ts:542`; `packages/server/src/combat/zone.ts:476` / `:511`.

The real ZoneManager reproduction retains the default 30-second sleep policy and three-tick catch-up limit. A real accepted 2 m upward movement crosses the ceiling; at 1041.05 shield becomes 60.2488, gaining 0.25 only 0.05 seconds after the fresh hit, instead of retaining the six-second delay.

The fixture uses a generated gas body through the manager's describe seam with a deterministic pressure layer, avoiding terrain-worker availability as a variable. No combat implementation, movement validator or scheduler is replaced. A control that services the overdue schedule while the zone is still asleep, then wakes and places the ship, retains the damage correctly after ascent.

Evidence: `wake-probe.test.ts`, `final-findings.log`, `wake-time.json`, `wake-health.json`. Two failing assertions (clock and actual shield), one passing ordering control. Original R1 direct runTick tests and the new permanent tests do not exercise this overdue advance ordering.

Fix boundary: reconcile wake with subsequent scheduled time, including frame-dependent consumers, and ensure earlier explicit damage times cannot overwrite newer hit authority. Preserving only the regeneration elapsed cursor is insufficient.
