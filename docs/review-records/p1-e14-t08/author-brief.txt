## Readable sources

`assets/blend/props/<stem>.blend`

`assets/blend/sites/outpost_0.blend`, `outpost_1.blend`, `outpost_2.blend`, `beacon_0.blend`, `beacon_1.blend`

Layout inside each file (art bible 8, `artlib/scene.py`):

- Scene units metric, scale 1.0. One Blender unit is one metre. +Z up. Apply transforms. Origin where the piece meets the ground.
- Top-level `REF`, hidden from export: `REF_Grid` (1 m, 20×20 m) and `REF_Biped` (1.85 m, feet at the origin).
- One top-level collection named the asset. Inside it, `<variant>_LOD0` through `<variant>_LOD3`.
- Markers sit beside the LODs. Shared markers live on the asset. A marker that belongs to one variant lives in that variant's collection.
- Collections starting with `_` are ignored work.

LOD0 is hand-authored. LOD3 is under 5% of that variant's LOD0 triangle count, and each LOD is lighter than the one before. If a lower LOD collection is absent, stock export decimates at ratios 1.0, 0.3, 0.1, 0.035. Those ratios satisfy the 5% rule. A hand-made LOD that is heavier than the one above it fails.

Materials are PBR for class `prop` and class `building`: base colour, roughness, metallic, normal, occlusion, and an emissive map. Link `assets/blend/_library.blend`. Do not edit it. The exporter bakes a linked library material into the asset. Glass, if used, is named `Glass` or `LIB_Glass` (constant colour and roughness; the glass profile does not require maps).

Textures: props 1024 px, `gpuTextureMB` 32 per variant. Sites (building) 2048 px, `gpuTextureMB` 128 per variant. Shipped maps are KTX2 through the stock exporter (normal maps UASTC, colour and ORM ETC1S) plus meshopt. Do not pass `--no-compress` into `packages/client/public/models`.

Names match `^[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9_-]*$`.

## Per-asset contract

**Prop class** (`families.json`): LOD0 2,000–10,000 triangles, longest side 0.05–6.00 m inclusive, 4 LODs, 1k maps. The props family does not require markers. These props still carry them, because the review overlay draws them and T07 will look them up by the same names:

- `COL_*` meshes, simple boxes, under 512 triangles each (over 512 warns).
- `Interact` or `Interact_*` empty at the use point, around knee to chest height on anything a person operates.
- Pods: `Interact` at the hatch. Stalls: `Interact` at the counter. Terminals: `Interact` in front of the panel. Ammo and vials: `Interact` on the carry handle. Debris: one `Interact` on each piece so a future salvage trace has a point; it does not make the piece a site.

Do not name a pod hatch or a stall flap `Door*`. The global reference rule fails any `Door*` mesh under 2.4 m tall and 1.4 m wide.

**`cargo_pod`.** Sealed ship cargo pod, Compact paint, longest side about 1.6–2.5 m (the crash-site box in `buildCrash` is 1.6×1.4×1.6 m). Hatch closed. A walk-in 6 m container is the outpost dressing in the generator (`container()`, 4.2–5.6 m) and is not this prop. Stay at or under 6.00 m including latches. Detail the shell, seams, and closed hatch enough to clear 2,000 triangles without a subdivided box.

**`loot_pod`.** The same family of pod, hatch open, cargo frame visible, dropped. Reaches or weathered Compact paint is enough difference. Own mesh, own file, so the review can load it alone.

**`terminal`.** Field terminal a person can stand at. Panel centre near 1.4–1.5 m (eye height is 1.70 m). Two variants, same massing, Compact paint and Reaches paint. This is not `prop_thalune_terminal`, `prop_ixari_terminal`, or a Waystation fabricator module.

**`market_stall`.** Counter stall, awning, longest side under 6 m, counter about 0.9–1.1 m. Two paints, same massing. This is not `prop_thalune_stall` or `prop_ixari_stall`. People-language stalls stay in the city kits.

**`ammo`.** Carry containers for the two ammo items, not the Flak Cannon or Torpedo Rack meshes (those are weapon modules, another task). `ammo_flak`: a fragment-round can about 0.3–0.6 m. `ammo_torpedo`: one torpedo in a handling cradle, longest side under 2 m. A bare cartridge smaller than 0.05 m fails the prop size floor, and a smooth bullet padded to 2,000 triangles fails the placeholder rubric.

**`catalyst_vial`.** Four Chorus vials, one mesh family, four material variants. Chorus palette: indigo `#2B2A5C`, parchment `#E9DFC7`, chant gold `#E8C66A`. About 0.2–0.35 m including the cap. Etch the catalyst's material character (frost, storm fracture, gamma clouding, acid bloom) in the glass and the liquid. Do not use glyph-light cyan.

**`crash_debris`.** The kit that replaces the procedural crash dressing in `buildCrash`: scatter boxes, a torus rim about 18 m across, and a small cargo box. The ship wreck stays the hull model. Do not author a Lark, Swift, Kestrel, or Mule, and do not author one crash site per hull.

- `crash_debris_plate` and `crash_debris_shard`: torn hull plate and a smaller shard, Compact and Reaches paint as worn metal, each under 3 m.
- `crash_debris_rim`: one scorched rim segment. The generator's full ring is about 18 m and cannot be one prop. A segment's longest side stays ≤ 6.00 m.
- `crash_debris_scorch`: one ground-scar slab, longest side ≤ 6.00 m.

The four pieces share an origin at the ground contact so a later placement can instance them. They are not parented into one 18 m assembly.

**Sites, building class:** LOD0 8,000–60,000 triangles, longest side 1–250 m, 4 LODs, 2k maps. Every variant requires at least one `COL_*` mesh and one `Interact` empty (`families.json` sites `requiredMarkers`). `Light_*` empties, when present, must have custom properties `color`, `intensity`, and `distance`. `Blink` and `Blink_*` must be empties with a child mesh (the lens). `Spin` is for monoliths; do not add it here.

**`outpost_0`.** Rebuild of the generator's dome shelter. Compact architecture from art bible 2.4: a lapped Thalune-style shell over an Ulgani-style lattice frame, with Ixari crystal windows. Abandoned in a hurry (DESIGN chapter 10), not a clean prefab and not generic geodesic sci-fi. Landing pad 24 m across (art bible 1, Tier 0–1). Steps 0.18 m rise and 0.30 m going, or a ramp, from the ground onto the pad. The calibration failure was a ~12–14 m pad on a ~2 m plinth with no steps. Service door mesh named `Door*` at least 2.4×1.4 m, and a person can walk to `Interact` in front of the terminal. `COL_*` covers the shelter and the pad edge, not the whole 24 m as one solid. Longest side stays inside 1–250 m; a 24 m pad plus a short approach is inside that.

**`outpost_1`.** Rebuild of the generator's prefab module deck. Same Compact language, same 24 m pad, same door and `Interact` contract, a different silhouette (modules on a deck, not a dome).

**`outpost_2`.** The third outpost. No generator twin. Same pad, door, collider, and `Interact` contract, third silhouette (a different massing of the same Compact parts). Not a monolith, not a drone, not a station module.

**`beacon_0`.** Rebuild of the square-platform mast. The generator puts `Light_1` at z = 18.2 m, so the mast is a building, not a prop. Keep a tall signal mast on a ground platform with steps. `Blink` at the lamp with a child lens mesh. Lamp material named `Lamp` (sites profile `signal`: it must emit and does not need PBR maps). Emission is Compact signal orange `#F28C28`. `Interact` at the ground console. `COL_*` on the mast and the platform.

**`beacon_1`.** Rebuild of the hexagonal-base spire. The generator's core runs to z = 9.2 m and continues as a spire, so it is also a building. Different silhouette from `beacon_0`. Same `Blink`, `Lamp`, `Interact`, and `COL_*` contract.

Glyph light is core `#D8F6FF` and halo `#7FD8FF`, and art bible 3.4 reserves it for the monolith network. These outposts, beacons, pods, stalls, terminals, ammo, vials, and debris do not use it. Monoliths and anchor stones are P1.E10.T08.

## Review consumer (later capture, not this unit)

`packages/client/src/dev/review.js` `parseSpec` loads `/models/<family>/<file>.glb` for `#review:<family>/<file>[:<asset>[/<variant>]]`. Roots are `<asset>/<variant>/<variant>_LOD<n>`, with `COL_*` and marker empties beside the LODs. No gameplay loader is involved.

When a capture is separately admitted, the stock command is:

`pnpm capture -- --review <family>/<stem> [--asset <asset>] [--variant <variant>]`

That writes `assets/renders/review/<family>/<stem>/` (or `<asset>/<variant>/` when a file holds more than one): noon-lush, night-exotic, and open-space, at LOD0 and LOD2, wireframe at both, `overlay.png`, `far_lod2.png` at 100 m, and a paired perf readout. Turntables are `pnpm turntable -- <file.glb> [out_dir] --family`. The grader's input is plan 0.10.3. PASS is every rubric score at least 4 and a mean of at least 4.5.

`--review node/<resource>` is the flora resource-node path. Do not use it for these meshes.

## Schema facts the author has to satisfy

These are validator and bible limits. They do not need a `families.json` edit.

1. Props and sites are different classes. Prop longest side is 0.05–6.00 m and LOD0 is 2,000–10,000 triangles. Building longest side is 1–250 m and LOD0 is 8,000–60,000 triangles. The 24 m pad and both beacon masts (18.2 m lamp, 9.2 m core plus spire) fail as props. A vial, ammo can, stall, pod, or debris segment fails as a building on the 8,000-triangle floor, and anything under 1 m also fails the building size floor. `asset_class` can override a variant's class, and this inventory does not use that override. Each file stays in the family whose default class matches the piece.
2. The prop triangle floor is 2,000 per variant. A smooth vial or a single round will miss it, or will hit it by subdivision and then fail the placeholder rubric. Author the carry object described above.
3. The generator crater is one ring about 18 m across. One mesh of that ring fails the prop maximum. The kit ships rim and scorch segments at or under 6.00 m. Do not widen the prop class.
4. `Door*` anywhere in the file must be at least 2.4×1.4 m. Outpost doors use that name. Pod hatches, stall flaps, and beacon console lids do not.
5. A material named `Lamp*` on a sites asset uses the signal profile: it must emit, and it does not take PBR maps. Painted surfaces stay on PBR names (`Hull`, `Accent`, `Trim`, `Dark`, or library names that are not `Lamp*`).
6. A `COL_*` mesh over 512 triangles warns. Keep colliders as boxes.
7. `Light_*` without `color`, `intensity`, and `distance` fails when the empty exists. `Blink` without a child mesh fails.
8. Size is measured on LOD0 render meshes only (`checks.py` `_size`). Marker empties do not count. A latch or lens that pushes the longest side past 6.00 m on a prop fails. The test is inclusive (`lo <= val <= hi`).
