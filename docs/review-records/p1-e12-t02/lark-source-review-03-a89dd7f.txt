Damaged contact luminance is 0.388, about 48% of the intact contact. The darkening lives on the damaged state. Hand-painted wear remains a visual-rubric item (`IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:116`).

Hull metallic is ~0.003–0.066 and hull roughness is ~0.39, so that metal rule does not fire. Compact's own target `#F4F4F0` is already near the ceiling. Worth a visual look, not a source block.
