However, repeated rectangular plates and clean lifted panel edges make destruction look assembled rather than fractured. **Change:** add localized broken crystal edges, torn attachment points, and exposed structure beneath selected missing plates.
- **Topology/LODs — 3:** Major outline survives reduction, but LOD2 changes canopy highlights markedly, collapses the rear crystal tip, and substantially erodes landing-foot shapes. **Change:** preserve canopy facet normals and those small silhouette features during reduction, then demonstrate transitions at intended distances.

- **Fitness — 4:** Stills show three deployed gear assemblies, open/closed canopy, seated occupant, boarding ladder, and two underside mounts with forward axes. Overlay volumes broadly follow the hull. This score covers visible arrangement only.

Five of nine paired measurements are marked unreliable. **Change:** obtain identified-device, reliable whole-scene evidence meeting 16.67 ms across all required cases; investigate the scene bottleneck before attributing it to Swift.
