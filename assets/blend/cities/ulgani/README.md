## Source conventions

- Metres; Blender +Z up, front -Y; GLB +Y up, front +Z. Ground contact is the placement origin. Raised walking surfaces carry their actual height in metadata.
- Each asset has four saved mesh LOD collections and separately named collision/point markers. LOD3 targets 3.5%; the authoritative ceiling is strictly below 5%.
- Structural mesh vertex groups keep every rib, membrane, repair, gill and root selectable. LOD0 is editable mesh data; LODs are reduced copies with UVs/normals retained.
- The local atlas material derives from the verified shared library's Chitin, Membrane, CapFlesh and Basalt conventions. Packed BaseColor, ORM, tangent Normal and Emissive images are 2k for buildings/kit, 1k for props and 4k for landmarks. ORM channels are painted occlusion, roughness and zero metallic. The eight atlas regions are chitin, membrane, cap, gill, root, walkway, basalt and spores. Paint follows growth and joints; there is no random-noise detail layer. Atlases are shared by each source's assets; no runtime procedural material is required.
- Ground corridors reserve at least 3 m width and 3.2 m headroom. Service entrances reserve 3.4 × 3.4 m. Doors remain open geometry. Raised shelves use a 0.18 m entry step, with full-width connection tongues. Bridges carry level decks and nominal 1.05 m handrails. Pads are 24/36 m diameter.
- `Interact_*` and `Light_*` are real empty markers beside the LODs. Their metadata records intended service roles and explicit integration-pending status. A marker does not implement its service.

## Reproduction and resources

Use `packages/tools/blender/cities/ulgani/pipeline.py --stage <stage> --groups <comma-list> --attempt <fresh-id>` from this checkout. Stages are `author`, `source-audit`, `export`, `audit` and `preview`. Author is an explicit destructive source revision, never an export prerequisite. Audits require an inventory whose SHA-256 matches the saved source. The other stages consume that saved source.

Every Blender operation goes through `run.py`, using factory/background `-t 2`, thread limits of two, one serialized codec invocation, exclusive lane locking, finite wall/RSS/log limits and process-group TERM/KILL/reap. Start gates require load ≤19, reported free memory ≥25% and ≥8 GiB free disk. Each attempt owns its scratch directory and immutable logs/receipts. A failed export removes only its named owned GLB. Other workers' files/processes are never cleaned.

`audit.py` reopens real sources and decoded compressed exports, invokes stock validation, measures every LOD, map route, collider and marker, and checks route clearance/support against both visible and collision triangles. `decode_meshopt.mjs` reads the actual normalized Meshopt attribute buffers with their world transforms. Every exported corner is matched jointly to a source position, UV and normal. This avoids Blender's reconstruction of tiny compressed faces changing the normals being measured. The original failing importer comparison is preserved alongside the direct-buffer pass.

Raw source vertices, face-referenced vertices, exported vertices and Blender-imported vertices are reported separately. Decimation leaves unused source vertices that have no exported surface. Counts are never silently equated. Walk-route tests sample three points across each corridor, approximately every 0.8 m, with a 0.18 m step allowance and 10 mm connector inset. A 5 mm support footprint handles measured sub-millimetre quantization seams, well below the current 0.4 m player radius. These sampled CPU checks do not replace native swept-player traversal. Collider fit uses actual nearest visible surface distances, including maxima and the P95 gate.

`negative_controls.py` mutates only in-memory copies or isolated temporary GLBs. Fifteen controls prove rejection of missing collision/LOD/UV/light metadata, wrong reference scale/class, uniform normals, missing required codec extensions, short mips, mismatched paired attributes, a blocking wall, a removed walkway floor and a failed export's stale output. Originals are hash-checked before and after.

`native-output-inventory.json` lists every future output, including 100 m far views, additional 200 m hero views, a family sheet and paired performance provenance. It creates no grant. The proposal is batches of four assets (44 ordinary images; at most 47 with hero/family additions), bounded by 48 retained images and 512 MiB. Parent-controlled archival must first account for this delivery's 48 CPU images. CPU turntables and map sheets are evidence of authoring iteration, not native visual acceptance.
