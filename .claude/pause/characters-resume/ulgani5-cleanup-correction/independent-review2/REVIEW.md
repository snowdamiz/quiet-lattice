## P2 — Reap completed direct children before signaling their groups

`packages/tools/blender/characters/capture_ulgani5.py:137-138` (`cleanup()`; related entry path at 171-172 and 184-190).

`cleanup()` calls `os.killpg()` before polling/reaping completed direct children. On macOS, signaling a group containing only an exited, unreaped direct `caffeinate` child raises `PermissionError(1, 'Operation not permitted')`. The code catches only `ProcessLookupError`; polling at line 143 is never reached, so cleanup aborts before the remaining groups can complete SIGKILL escalation.

This is reachable through the actual normal-path wait structure: when the capture's Node process exits, `caffeinate -w <node>` can exit too. If `sampler.wait(timeout=5)` times out, the combined try skips `assertion.wait(timeout=5)`. Cleanup then sees the unreaped assertion. Its first failure sends execution into the outer exception handler, whose retry fails on the same assertion; the handler records `drain_error` and exits while a live sampler or other owned descendants can remain.

Independent real-process reproduction used AST-extracted, otherwise unchanged candidate `processes/save/observe/cleanup` functions, a real `/usr/bin/caffeinate -i -w <target>` that exited after its watched direct child, and a live SIGTERM-resistant direct child with a resistant descendant. The five-second sampler wait timed out and skipped the assertion wait. Both cleanup invocations raised EPERM, neither reached SIGKILL, and `ps` still showed the live direct child and descendant. In that fixture the live group received SIGTERM twice and remained live. An exited-direct-only control also reproduced EPERM; a live-only resistant group control successfully drained through TERM then KILL. These establish a resource leak in the exact helper rather than assuming the analogous station failure applies.

Fix by reaping completed direct children before selecting/signaling groups, and ensuring an already-exited group cannot abort draining the remaining verified-owned resources. Do not treat PermissionError as evidence that a live group is absent. Add a real Darwin regression covering the skipped assertion wait, repeat cleanup, and a resistant owned descendant. Preserve the existing frozen packet and failure history.

Evidence: `cleanup-fixtures.py`, `cleanup-fixtures.json`, `cleanup-entry-fixture.py`, `cleanup-entry-fixture.json`, and `cleanup-normal-node-exit-sampler-timeout/fixture-result.json` in this directory.
